# Registered choice-inference review

The 6,768 Swissmetro tasks come from752 people, each answering several questions. Counting every answer as independent can make coefficients look more certain than the respondent population justifies. This optional repair keeps the original fixed four-parameter choice specification and computes uncertainty three ways: inverse information under model/row independence, a robust row-score sandwich, and a respondent-score sandwich allowing dependence within each person's answers.

Method/input registration preceded the new inference execution. Exact source, sample and environment are in `research_inference/INPUT_SOURCE.json`; all historical core/notebook/four CSV hashes are in `HISTORICAL_SHA256.json`. The analytic score is chosen attributes minus probability-weighted attributes. Information sums their probability-weighted within-choice covariance. Respondent scores sum rows withinID; cluster covariance is inverse-information × sum of respondent-score outer products × inverse-information, with the preregistered G/(G−1)*(N−1)/(N−K) factor. Respondents are assumed independent. These assumptions do not establish realistic fare causality or population calibration.

The independent analytic likelihood matches the official fixed-specification Biogeme benchmark−5331.252. Conventional and row-sandwich SE agree with the original/official displayed values, respectively. Respondent-cluster SE increase about3 times over conventional SE. The Car constant changes from a historical conventional p≈0.000348 to a cluster normal-reference p≈0.231. The time and cost coefficients retain their signs and small asymptotic p-values. All adverse results are reported; no model, sample or criterion was tuned after observing this comparison.

The trust-exact optimizer's failure/precision termination flag remains visible, with absolute gradient infinity norm2.10e-8 satisfying the preregistered<=1e-6 criterion and full-rank information. This is mathematical stationarity under the stated criterion, not a rewritten success flag. Thirteen substantive tests check analytic fixtures/derivatives, malformed inputs, grouping and utility invariance, singularity, corruption, exact typed-reference semantics and refusal to overwrite historical or existing outputs. Subsequent full result replays require exact source/count/schema identities and only finite float roundoff within1e-8 relative/absolute tolerance.

An isolated execution of the unchanged historical core source uses these registered bytes in its existing loader and reproduces the point, respondent-holdout and pricing CSVs within registered1e-6 tolerance. No old file changes; the old HTTP data acquisition is not tested by this computational replay. New outputs live in a new caller-selected directory. The frozen summary and dedicated workflow are linked in the README. This is a reproducible methodological correction on a known stated-preference dataset, not a new independent holdout, real-market price response, commercial recommendation, external endorsement or assessed personal mastery.
